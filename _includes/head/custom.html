It was #1a1a2e — a navy from the design before this one, so Android Chrome and iOS Safari painted the browser chrome navy above a warm-ivory page: the one place on a phone where the site contradicted its own colour decision. These are the two grounds the token file actually defines. Media-scoped theme-color follows the OS preference rather than the site's own toggle, so a reader who has forced dark on a light OS still gets a light chrome bar. That is a browser limitation — there is no way to drive this from data-theme — and it is strictly better than a colour from neither theme. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} THE SHARE CARD.
